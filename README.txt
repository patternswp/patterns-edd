Patterns EDD is a clean and modern block-based WordPress theme designed for digital product stores and online creators using Easy Digital Downloads (EDD). It provides all the tools you need to build a professional and user-friendly eCommerce site for selling digital goods like software, eBooks, music, or design assets.

== Description ==

Patterns EDD is a sleek and efficient Full Site Editing (FSE) WordPress theme, ideal for creating a professional online store for selling digital products. Built with WordPress FSE, this theme offers easy customization of headers, footers, templates, and global styles directly within the Site Editor.

It features pre-designed patterns and layouts tailored for showcasing digital products, product categories, customer testimonials, pricing tables, team sections, and contact forms. Its fully responsive design ensures your store looks polished and works flawlessly across all devices.

Whether you're selling eBooks, software, music, or design assets, Patterns EDD delivers a powerful platform to reach and engage your customers.
